Simple Process

How Skillmesh Works

Four straightforward steps to trade knowledge and accelerate your personal growth.

1

Create your profile.

Sign up using your college email. Outline your academic focus, interests, and availability for study sessions.

2

Add skills you can teach.

List the topics you feel confident sharing—whether it's Java syntax, Figma layouts, or statistics.

3

Choose skills you want to learn.

Select the subjects or technologies you want to master. No tuition fees or commercial subscriptions required.

4

Connect with matching students.

Skillmesh identifies mutual matches where your strengths align with another student's learning targets.
